# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-focused. Critique code, claims, tests, and designs without attacking the person who contributed them. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about contributors or users.

Security reports and disagreements about research evidence should be handled with care: preserve confidentiality when needed, distinguish measured results from opinion, and correct mistakes transparently.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when behavior makes collaboration unsafe or persistently disruptive.
